**The first login starts on a finished desk.** A machine with no settings yet
opens on the *Moon castle* profile: the whole bar in one capsule, Analogue
widgets on the wallpaper and the dock at the bottom. It types in the machine's
keyboard layout, the one `localectl` wrote in
`/etc/X11/xorg.conf.d/00-keyboard.conf`. Everything below changes it.

## The keys to know

| Keys | What they do |
|---|---|
| <kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>Return</kbd> | Open a terminal |
| <kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>Space</kbd> | The launcher |
| <kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>Q</kbd> | Close the focused window |
| <kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>1</kbd> … <kbd>0</kbd> | Go to a workspace, on the screen you are on; add <kbd>SHIFT</kbd> to move the window there |
| <kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>ALT</kbd> <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> | Go to the other screen; add <kbd>SHIFT</kbd> to take the workspace with you |
| <kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>TAB</kbd> | Every workspace at once |
| <kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> | The control centre |
| <kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>X</kbd> | The session menu |
| <kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>L</kbd> | Lock the screen |
| <kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>H</kbd> | Every other key |

A panel closes with the key that opened it, a click anywhere else, or
<kbd>Escape</kbd>. The full list is in [Keybindings](/reference/keybindings/).

## Change the wallpaper

<Steps>

1. Press <kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd>. The island becomes a strip of the
   wallpapers.

2. Slide the strip with <kbd>Left</kbd> and <kbd>Right</kbd>, or with the
   pointer, and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> on the one in the middle.

3. Press <kbd>Down</kbd> for the palettes under it, or open them directly with
   <kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>SHIFT</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd>. The adaptive palette takes
   its colours from the wallpaper; the others are fixed.

</Steps>

The same panel is under **Wallpaper** and **Palette** when you right-click the
wallpaper, and in Settings → Appearance → Theme. To add your own pictures, put
them in `~/.local/share/wallpapers` (JPEG, PNG or WebP). They appear the next
time the strip opens. More in [Wallpapers and palettes](/theming/palettes/).

## Open Settings

Press <kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>,</kbd>, or right-click the wallpaper and choose
**Settings**. It is an ordinary window with a search field over the sidebar,
and the sidebar is in three groups: the shell, the desk and the session.

## Arrange the bar

Open Settings → Bar & Island → **The bar**. The bar is drawn as a picture, with
a catalogue of every piece under it. Drag a piece onto the left or right half
of the bar; drag one off the bar to remove it. Click a piece for a look of its
own. **Style**, on the same page, draws the same layout grouped around the
island, spread to the edges, or inside one capsule. See [The bar](/shell/bar/).

## Arrange the desk

<Steps>

1. Right-click the wallpaper and choose **Arrange widgets**. The widgets come in
   front of the windows, and a card of every module opens at the bottom.

2. Drag a piece from the card onto the grid. Pull a widget's corner to change
   its shape, and click it for its own look.

3. Take a widget off with the badge in its corner, or by dropping it back on the
   card.

4. Press <kbd>Escape</kbd>, or right-click the grid, to finish.

</Steps>

Settings → Desktop → The widgets → **Edit** opens the same mode. See
[Desktop](/shell/desktop/).

## Switch profile

A profile keeps a whole desk under a name: the bar, the widgets, the dock, the
look, the keys, the wallpaper and its palette. Three come with impasto:
*Moon castle*, *Fuji* and *Night bay*. Open Settings → System → **Profiles** and
press **Switch** on one.

<Aside type="caution">
Switching re-reads the Hyprland configuration, so anything you changed by hand
with `hyprctl` goes with it.
</Aside>

Your screens, your name and picture, the language, the weather location and the
GitHub user stay with the machine. Notes, tasks and the clipboard are shared by
every profile. See [Settings](/shell/settings/).

## Find every other key

<kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>H</kbd> shows every key on one sheet in the island,
read from the compositor, so a key you changed is on it the next time it opens.
To change one, go to Settings → Keys: **The shell's own** and **The
compositor's**. Every key belongs to the profile in use. See
[Keys and packages](/shell/keys-and-packages/).
