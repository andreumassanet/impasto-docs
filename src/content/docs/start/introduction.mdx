**impasto is a desktop shell for Hyprland, with the rest of the desk around it.**
The shell is written in [Quickshell](https://quickshell.org) and is most of the
code. The rest is the terminal, the prompt, two editors, two file managers, a
browser, a chat client and the login screen, all configured to match. One
script, `setup`, installs all of it on Arch Linux.

<Figure name="hero" alt="The desk: the island at the top with a track playing, widgets down the right, a deck of notes on the left edge, a terminal with the lava lamp greeting, and the dock at the bottom" caption="The island, the widgets, a note deck on the edge, the terminal and the dock." />

The name is the painting technique the wallpapers imitate: paint laid on thick
enough to keep the mark of the brush. The desk comes with forty AI-generated
images in that style.

## What it needs

- **Arch Linux.** Every package is named in `packages/pacman.txt` and
  `packages/aur.txt`, and `setup` installs them with pacman and yay or paru.
- **Hyprland 0.56 or newer, configured in Lua.** It is tested on 0.56.2.
- **Quickshell 0.3**, installed with the rest.
- **A terminal inside the Hyprland session** to run the installer from, and
  your password for sudo.

Most features rely on packages that are optional. A missing one takes its own
control away rather than failing.
