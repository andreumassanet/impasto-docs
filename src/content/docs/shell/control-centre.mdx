**The control centre is a grid of blocks under one row of buttons.** It opens
with <kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd>, with a click on the island at rest or on
its glance, or with the Control centre button if it is on the bar. The same key
closes it, and so do Escape and a click anywhere off the island.

## The top row

**Small buttons over the grid, on a left and a right side.** Out of the box,
the session's actions are on the left — Lock, Suspend, Log out, Restart, Shut
down — and doors to other panels on the right: System statistics, Settings,
Pet, Games, Notes and Task board. Lock and Suspend run at once; the other three
turn red on the first click and run on the second.

Any button can go on either side. The doors on offer are System statistics,
Settings, Pet, Games, Notes, Task board, Workspace overview, Launcher,
Appearance, Session menu, Keys and Packages. With both sides empty there is no
row, and no space for one.

- **In Settings → Control Centre → The top row**, the row is drawn in
  miniature with a tray of the buttons on neither side. Drag a button from the
  tray onto a side, along it, across to the other, or back into the tray to
  take it off.
- **While arranging**, click the row as you would a block. Its card lists every
  button with a tick for the ones on the row, the side each is on — a click
  moves it across — and arrows to order it along that side. An empty row shows
  while arranging, so it can be filled.

## The grid

**A grid of blocks, and every block takes a whole number of cells.** It is six
columns by eight rows out of the box, and two to six columns by two to eight
rows can be chosen. A cell is one toggle tile, wider than it is tall, so the
square sizes are 1×2 and 2×4. Blocks never overlap, and a block can be on the
grid more than once.

Out of the box: the toggles, the volume and brightness sliders, the appearance
card and the session's buttons down the left; the player, the weather, the
month and a row of shortcuts in the middle; the notifications down the right.

| Block | Sizes (columns × rows) | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| Toggles | 2×2, 2×3, 2×4, 3×2, 3×3, 4×2, 4×3 | Switches and one-press actions, in pages (below) |
| Volume | 2×1, 3×1, 4×1 | The output's slider; its chevron opens [the sound page](#the-pages) |
| Brightness | 2×1, 3×1, 4×1 | The slider for the screen the keyboard is on; with several screens, a chevron opens one for each |
| Appearance | 2×2, 2×3, 2×4, 3×3, 4×2 | The wallpaper; a click opens the appearance panel |
| Media | 2×2, 2×3, 3×2, 4×2 | The player |
| Weather | 2×1, 2×2, 2×3, 4×2 | The conditions now, and the rest of the day when there is room |
| Calendar | 2×3, 2×4, 3×4 | The month, a dot under each day with tasks; such a day turns into its list |
| Notifications | 3×2, 3×3, 2×4, 3×4, 2×6, 2×8, 3×8 | The history, with Clear |
| Session | 1×1, 2×1, 3×1, 1×2, 2×2, 4×1 | Round buttons for Lock, Suspend, Log out, Restart and Shut down |
| Shortcuts | 1×1, 2×1, 3×1, 1×2, 2×2, 4×1, 6×1 | Round buttons that open other panels, chosen per block |
| impasto | 1×2, 2×2, 2×4 | The terminal greeting in miniature: the palette board and the machine's lines |
| Pet | 2×2, 2×3 | The pet's face and mood, feed and play; three rows add the shelf |
| Clock | 1×2, 2×2, 2×4 | The time over the day, larger with the room |
| Games | 2×1, 2×2 | The last best and Play; two rows, the last three games |
| Notes | 1×2, 2×2, 2×4 | The front note of the deck; a click opens it |
| Tasks | 1×2, 2×2, 2×3, 2×4 | How many are left and what is next; larger, the next few with Open |

A block with nothing to show — the player with no music — draws its empty card
rather than leaving the cell. Session and shortcuts blocks hold as many
buttons as their cells fit.

## The toggles

**One block, in pages.** A page is what fits the block; the rest go on further
pages, turned with the wheel, a sideways drag, the arrows or the dots along the
bottom. The six out of the box are Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Power, Focus, Microphone
and Airplane.

| Tile | What it does |
|---|---|
| Wi-Fi | Switches the radio. Its chevron opens the Wi-Fi list. |
| Bluetooth | Switches Bluetooth. Its chevron opens the device list. |
| Power | Switches the power profile between balanced and performance; lit on performance |
| Focus | Do not disturb — notifications stay off the island |
| Microphone | Mutes the microphone. Its chevron opens [its page](#the-pages). |
| Airplane | Wi-Fi and Bluetooth off together, or back on |
| Night light | A warmer screen, through hyprsunset. Its chevron opens [its page](#the-pages). |
| Output | Mutes the sound |
| Dock | Shows or hides [the dock](/shell/dock/) |
| Notch | Cuts the island into the top edge, or floats it |
| Shadows | Shadows on the windows |
| Game mode | Stills the shell and drops Hyprland's animations, blur, shadows and glass; see [game mode](/shell/settings/#game-mode) |
| Capture | Opens [the capture surface](/shell/capture/) on its last choices |
| Annotate | A region, straight into satty |
| Read text | A region, read as text onto the clipboard |
| Colour | The colour of one pixel, onto the clipboard |
| Record | Records the screen the keyboard is on, at once, or stops the recording |
| Clear clipboard | Empties the clipboard history; only there while something is kept |

The last six close the panel first, so it is not in the picture. A tile the
machine cannot do — no Bluetooth adapter, no satty, no hyprpicker — is dimmed
and does nothing.

**The Wi-Fi list** shows the networks in range with their signal, which are
secured and which are saved, a Rescan and the switch. A click on a secured
network opens a password field in its row. **The Bluetooth list** shows the
devices that have a name, and anything paired or connected; the unnamed ones
are folded under a line at the bottom. A click connects. Both lists also open
from the Network and Bluetooth pieces on [the bar](/shell/bar/).

<Aside>
Bluetooth connects but cannot pair a new device, and Wi-Fi cannot forget a
saved network. Pair and forget with `bluetoothctl` and `nmcli`. See
[limitations](/help/limitations/).
</Aside>

## The pages

**A chevron opens a page of its own in the island**, as the Wi-Fi and
Bluetooth tiles open their lists. The back arrow at the top returns to the
control centre.

- **Sound**, from the volume slider: the output's level, a row for every
  application playing with its own level, and the outputs to choose from.
- **Microphone**, from its tile: the input's level, every application
  recording it with its own level, and the inputs to choose from.
- **Night light**, from its tile: the warmth as a level, and four presets —
  Candle, Warm, Soft and Neutral, 2500 K to 5500 K. The tile itself is the
  switch.
- **Brightness**, from its slider when there is more than one screen to dim: a
  level for each screen, by name. With one screen there is no chevron.

## Arranging

<Steps>

1. Right-click the panel's margin or the space between blocks, or open
   Settings → Control Centre and press **Edit**. The grid's cells show, a pill
   under the island sets its columns and rows, and a card of every block, each
   at the smallest size it offers, opens under them.

2. Drag a block from the card onto a cell, or drag a block on the grid to move
   it. The cell it will land on lights before you let go. A click on a block on
   the card puts it in the first free place.

3. Pull a block's corner, or scroll over it, to step through the sizes it
   offers.

4. Take a block off with its ⊖ badge, or by dropping it on the card.

5. Click a block for its card: its name, Remove, and its sizes. On a toggles
   block the card lists every tile with a tick, and arrows to order the ones
   ticked; on a shortcuts block it does the same for the doors. So two blocks
   of either kind can carry two different sets. Click the top row for
   [its card](#the-top-row).

6. Press <kbd>Escape</kbd>, right-click the margin again, or close the panel.
   A second <kbd>Escape</kbd> closes the island.

</Steps>

<Figure name="control-centre-arrange" alt="The control centre in arranging mode, its grid's cells showing, with a card under the island holding every block at its smallest size: toggles, appearance, calendar, notifications, clock, notes, tasks, media, weather, pet, games, volume and brightness." caption="Arranging, with the card of every block under it." />

Columns come and go on both sides alike, so the blocks stay centred.

The card of blocks moves, grows and scrolls as the desktop's does
([Arranging the desktop](/shell/desktop/#arranging)).

The columns and rows are also under Settings → Control Centre → The panel.
**Default layout**, beside Edit there, puts the grid back as it came.
