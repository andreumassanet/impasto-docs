**The control centre is a grid of blocks under one row of buttons.** It opens
with <kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd>, with a click on the island at rest or on
its glance, or with the Control centre button if it is on the bar. The same key
closes it, and so do Escape and a click anywhere off the island.

## The top row

**Small buttons over the grid, on its right, and the time and the battery on
its left.** Out of the box the row holds the session's actions — Lock,
Suspend, Log out, Restart, Shut down — then doors to other panels: System
statistics, Settings, Pet, Games, Notes and Task board. Lock and Suspend run at
once; the other three turn red on the first click and run on the second.

The doors on offer are System statistics, Settings, Pet, Games, Notes, Task
board, Workspace overview, Launcher, Appearance, Session menu, Keys, Packages,
Servers, Screen time and Camera. The time and the battery's charge are off out
of the box, and switched under Settings → Control Centre → Clicks, the clock
and the battery; the battery is offered only on a machine with one. With no
buttons and neither of the two there is no row, and no space for one.

- **In Settings → Control Centre → The top row**, the row is drawn in
  miniature with a tray of the buttons off it. Drag a button from the tray
  onto the row, along it, or back into the tray to take it off.
- **While arranging**, click the row as you would a block. Its card lists every
  button with a tick for the ones on the row, and arrows to order them along
  it. An empty row shows while arranging, so it can be filled.

## The grid

**A grid of blocks, and every block takes a whole number of cells.** It is six
columns by eight rows out of the box, and two to six columns by two to eight
rows can be chosen. A cell is one toggle tile, wider than it is tall, so the
square sizes are 1×2 and 2×4. Blocks never overlap, and a block can be on the
grid more than once.

Out of the box: the toggles, the volume and brightness sliders and the
appearance card down the left; the player, the weather and the month in the
middle; the notifications down the right.

| Block | Sizes (columns × rows) | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| Toggles | 2×2, 2×3, 2×4, 3×2, 3×3, 4×2, 4×3 | Switches and one-press actions, in pages (below) |
| Volume | 2×1, 3×1, 4×1 | The output's slider; its chevron opens [the sound page](#the-pages) |
| Brightness | 2×1, 3×1, 4×1 | The slider for the screen the keyboard is on; with several screens, a chevron opens one for each |
| Appearance | 2×2, 2×3, 2×4, 3×3, 4×2 | The wallpaper; a click opens the appearance panel |
| Media | 2×2, 2×3, 3×2, 4×2 | The player; a click on the cover opens the player's detail |
| Weather | 2×1, 2×2, 2×3, 4×2 | The conditions now, and the rest of the day when there is room; a click opens its detail |
| Calendar | 2×3, 2×4, 3×4 | The month, a dot under each day with [events](#calendar-events) or tasks; such a day turns into its hours and its list |
| Notifications | 3×2, 3×3, 2×4, 3×4, 2×6, 2×8, 3×8 | The history, with Clear |
| Session | 1×1, 2×1, 3×1, 1×2, 2×2, 4×1 | Round buttons for Lock, Suspend, Log out, Restart and Shut down |
| Shortcuts | 1×1, 2×1, 3×1, 1×2, 2×2, 4×1, 6×1 | Round buttons that open other panels, chosen per block |
| impasto | 1×2, 2×2, 2×4 | The terminal greeting in miniature: the palette board and the machine's lines |
| Pet | 2×2, 2×3 | The pet's face and mood, feed and play; three rows add the shelf |
| Clock | 1×2, 2×2, 2×4 | The time over the day, larger with the room |
| Games | 2×1, 2×2 | The last best and Play; two rows, the last three games |
| Notes | 1×2, 2×2, 2×4 | The front note of the deck; a click opens it |
| Tasks | 1×2, 2×2, 2×3, 2×4 | How many are left and what is next; larger, the next few with Open |

A block with nothing to show (the player with no music) draws its empty card
rather than leaving the cell. Session and shortcuts blocks hold as many
buttons as their cells fit.

## The toggles

**One block, in pages.** A page is what fits the block; the rest go on further
pages, turned with the wheel, a sideways drag, the arrows or the dots along the
bottom. The six out of the box are Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Power, Focus, Microphone
and Airplane.

A tile with a chevron toggles wherever it is pressed, and the chevron opens its
page. With **Menu on the label** on, under Settings → Control Centre → Clicks,
the clock and the battery, the icon toggles and the rest of the tile opens the
page.

| Tile | What it does |
|---|---|
| Wi-Fi | Switches the radio. Its chevron opens the Wi-Fi list. |
| Bluetooth | Switches Bluetooth. Its chevron opens the device list. |
| Power | Lit off balanced: a press goes from balanced to the last other profile picked, power saver until one is, and back. Its chevron opens [every profile](#the-pages). Needs power-profiles-daemon. |
| Focus | Do not disturb — notifications stay off the island |
| Microphone | Mutes the microphone. Its chevron opens [its page](#the-pages). |
| Keep awake | Holds off the idle lock, the screen going off and suspend while it is on; it is not kept across a restart of the shell |
| Airplane | Wi-Fi and Bluetooth off together, or back on |
| Night light | A warmer screen, through hyprsunset. Its chevron opens [its page](#the-pages). |
| Output | Mutes the sound |
| Dock | Shows or hides [the dock](/shell/dock/) |
| Notch | Cuts the island into the top edge, or floats it |
| Shadows | The shadows on the windows, the bar, the dock and the widgets, all at once |
| Game mode | Stills the shell, makes it solid, and drops Hyprland's animations, blur, shadows and refraction; see [game mode](/shell/settings/#game-mode) |
| Capture | Opens [the capture surface](/shell/capture/) on its last choices |
| Annotate | A region, straight into satty |
| Read text | A region, read as text onto the clipboard |
| Colour | The colour of one pixel, onto the clipboard |
| Record | Records the screen the keyboard is on, at once, or stops the recording. Its chevron opens [the recent takes](#the-pages). |
| Clear clipboard | Empties the clipboard history; only there while something is kept |
| Song | Listens to 12 seconds of what the speakers play and names the song in a notification, through songrec; listed only where songrec and parec are installed |

Capture, Annotate, Read text, Colour, Record, Clear clipboard and Song close
the panel first, so it is not in the picture. Song sends a short fingerprint of what is playing,
a call included, to Shazam. A tile the
machine cannot do (no Bluetooth adapter, no satty, no hyprpicker) is dimmed
and does nothing.

**Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and Sound are tabs of one panel**, along a strip at its
top: a click on a tab, or <kbd>Tab</kbd> and <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Tab</kbd>,
goes between them. **The Wi-Fi list** shows the networks in range with their
signal, which are secured and which are saved, a Rescan and the switch. A
click on a secured network opens a password field in its row. **The Bluetooth
list** shows the devices that have a name, and anything paired or connected;
the unnamed ones are folded under a line at the bottom. A click on a new
device pairs it, trusts it and connects it once paired; on a paired one it
connects or disconnects. The bin on a paired device under the pointer, or the
right button, forgets it. Both lists also open from the Network and Bluetooth
pieces on [the bar](/shell/bar/).

<Aside>
Wi-Fi cannot forget a saved network, and a Bluetooth device that asks for a
PIN cannot be paired here. Use `nmcli` and `bluetoothctl`. See
[limitations](/help/limitations/#network-and-bluetooth).
</Aside>

## The pages

**A chevron opens a page of its own in the island**, as the Wi-Fi and
Bluetooth tiles open their lists. The back arrow at the top returns to the
control centre.

- **Sound**, from the volume slider: the output's level, a row for every
  application playing with its own level, the outputs to choose from and,
  where there is a microphone, the inputs under them. With EasyEffects
  installed, **Sound effects row** under Settings → Island adds a row that
  says whether it is on, with a button for its window.
- **Microphone**, from its tile: the input's level, every application
  recording it with its own level, and the inputs to choose from.
- **Night light**, from its tile: the warmth as a level, and four presets,
  Candle, Warm, Soft and Neutral, 2500 K to 5500 K. The tile itself is the
  switch.
- **Brightness**, from its slider when there is more than one screen to dim: a
  level for each screen, by name. With one screen there is no chevron.
- **Power**, from its tile: one row per profile power-profiles-daemon offers,
  Power saver, Balanced and, where the machine has it, Performance. The
  battery's card on [the bar](/shell/bar/) switches the same three.
- **Record**, from its tile: the four newest takes, newest first, with the
  date and size of each, and a row that opens the folder. A click on a take
  plays it. A recording is stopped from the island, which shows it wherever
  you are.

### Volume and brightness past their ends

- **Volume above 100 %**, under Settings → Control Centre → Sound, lets the
  output, the input and every application go to 150 %. The sliders, the
  volume keys and the volume command all stop at the same place. Off out of
  the box: above 100 % the sound is amplified and can distort.
- **Dim below the backlight**, under Settings → Displays → The screen →
  Brightness, gives the slider and the keys a bottom fifth that darkens the
  picture past the backlight's lowest step, down to a quarter, through
  hyprsunset. Screenshots, recordings and the colour picker keep the true
  colours. It is kept by the machine, since it depends on the panel, and off
  out of the box.

## Calendar events

**The calendar shows the events of calendars you link, and of khal.** Under
Settings → Modules → Calendar, give a calendar a name and its iCalendar link
(an `https://` or `webcal://` address ending in `.ics`) and press **Add**:
Google's *Secret address in iCal format*, under the calendar's settings, or
Outlook's published ICS link under Shared calendars → Publish a calendar. Each
link's row says how many events are ahead, or why it could not be read, and
its bin removes it. Calendars already set up in khal show without a link.

- A day with events gets a dot, in the calendar block and in the desktop's
  calendar. A click on it shows the day as hours, each event a block from its
  start to its end, side by side where they overlap, with all-day events above
  and a line at the time now on today. Then the day's tasks.
- Links are read at most every fifteen minutes, repeating events included, and
  the last good copy is kept for when a download fails. Events are read only:
  nothing is written back.
- The link is private: anyone who has it can read the calendar. It is kept by
  the machine, not the profile.

## Arranging

<Steps>

1. Right-click the panel's margin or the space between blocks, or open
   Settings → Control Centre and press **Edit**. The grid's cells show. Left
   of the island, one card sets the grid's size and another edits the top
   row; on the right, a card holds every block at the smallest size it
   offers, grouped under Controls, At a glance and Personal. A screen with no
   room beside the island stacks them under it instead.

2. Drag a block from the card onto a cell, or drag a block on the grid to move
   it. The cell it will land on lights before you let go. A click on a block on
   the card puts it in the first free place.

3. Pull a block's corner, or scroll over it, to step through the sizes it
   offers.

4. Take a block off with its ⊖ badge, or by dropping it on the card.

5. Click a block for its card: its name, Remove, and its sizes. On a toggles
   block the card lists every tile with a tick, and arrows to order the ones
   ticked; on a shortcuts block it does the same for the doors. So two blocks
   of either kind can carry two different sets. Click the top row for
   [its card](#the-top-row).

6. Press <kbd>Escape</kbd>, right-click the margin again, or close the panel.
   A second <kbd>Escape</kbd> closes the island.

</Steps>

<Figure name="control-centre-arrange" alt="The control centre in arranging mode, its grid's cells showing, with a card under the island holding every block at its smallest size: toggles, appearance, calendar, notifications, clock, notes, tasks, media, weather, pet, games, volume and brightness." caption="Arranging, with the card of every block under it." />

**The size is picked on a miniature of the largest grid**, lit from the top
left: the cell clicked becomes the grid's far corner. Columns come and go on
both sides alike, so the blocks stay centred. The same miniature is under
Settings → Control Centre → The panel → **Size**, and **Default layout**,
beside Edit there, puts the grid back as it came.
