**One key opens a thing you can look at.** <kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>SHIFT</kbd>
<kbd>S</kbd> photographs the screen the keyboard is on and shows that
photograph over it, with a bar at its foot saying what is about to happen. You
draw on the photograph, and the crop is cut from the same pixels — so nothing
has to be frozen, and a menu that closes when the pointer moves is already in
the picture. So is a panel open in
the island: it waits under the surface, and is still open when the surface
closes.

## Keys

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>SHIFT</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd>, or <kbd>Print</kbd> | Opens the capture surface |
| <kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>SHIFT</kbd> <kbd>R</kbd> | Records the screen the keyboard is on, or stops the recording |
| <kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>SHIFT</kbd> <kbd>C</kbd> | Picks a colour off the screen |

The Capture, Annotate, Read text, Colour and Record tiles in
[the control centre](/shell/control-centre/) do the same, and so do the rows of
the same names under `>` in [the launcher](/shell/launcher/). The bar's
**Capture** button, placed from [the layout editor](/shell/bar/#arranging),
opens the surface as the key does.

## The surface

The bar at the foot draws every choice as a glyph, in three groups, and the
one under the pointer says its name above the bar:

| Group | Choices |
|---|---|
| Shape | **Region**, **Window**, **Screen** |
| Kind | **Photo**, **Video** |
| Then | For a photo: **Save**, **Copy**, **Annotate**, **Read text**. For a video: **No sound**, **Sound**. |

- **Region** — drag a box. Everything outside it is washed back, and its size
  rides above it. Letting go takes it.
- **Window** — the window under the pointer is outlined; a click takes it.
- **Screen** — a click takes the whole screen.

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>1</kbd> <kbd>2</kbd> <kbd>3</kbd> | Region, Window, Screen |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> | Takes the screen, or the window under the pointer |
| <kbd>Escape</kbd>, or the right button | Cancels, and nothing is kept |

It opens on the shape and kind you used last. Where a photo goes always starts
back at Save: annotating or reading text is chosen each time.

## What happens to a photo

| Choice | What happens |
|---|---|
| Save | Saved to the captures folder **and** copied to the clipboard; the island shows the file's name |
| Copy | Copied to the clipboard only |
| Annotate | Opens in satty. What you save from it goes to the captures folder, and its copy button puts it on the clipboard. |
| Read text | Read with tesseract and copied as text; the island says how many characters |

Annotate is only offered with satty installed, and Read text with tesseract. It
reads English, and Spanish too when its language pack (`tesseract-data-spa`)
is installed.

**The captures folder** is `XDG_SCREENSHOTS_DIR` when your `user-dirs.dirs`
names one, and otherwise a `Screenshots` folder inside your pictures folder.
Files are named by the moment they were taken, as
`2026-09-13-101500_impasto.png`.

## Recording

**<kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>SHIFT</kbd> <kbd>R</kbd> records the screen the
keyboard is on, at once, and the same key stops it.** The Record tile in the
control centre does the same. A region or a window is recorded from the
surface: choose **Video**, then draw the box or click the window, and the take
starts; **Screen** in Video records that whole screen.

- While it runs, a red dot and the minutes sit beside the time on
  [the island](/shell/island/). A click on the dot stops it; so do the key and
  the Record tile.
- **Sound** is chosen on the surface, beside Video, and holds for the key and
  the tile too. It records what the machine plays, not the microphone, and is
  only there when there is an output to record from.
- The take is written with wl-screenrec, or with wf-recorder when wl-screenrec
  is missing or will not start, as an mp4 named like
  `2026-09-13-101500_impasto.mp4` — in `XDG_SCREENCASTS_DIR`, or a
  `Screencasts` folder inside your videos folder.
- The island says *Recording* when it starts and *Recorded* with the length
  when it stops. A recording keeps going if the shell is restarted, and the
  shell picks it back up.

The recording is of the live screen: the box is where it records, not the
photograph it was drawn on.

## Colour

<kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>SHIFT</kbd> <kbd>C</kbd> freezes the screen with
hyprpicker; click a pixel and its hex code goes onto the clipboard, and the
island shows it.

## Keys for one shape

Five more captures are registered and bound to nothing: **Capture a region**,
**Capture a window**, **Capture the screen**, **Capture and annotate** and
**Read a region**. Each opens the surface already set to that shape and
destination. Give any of them a key under Settings → Keys → *The shell's own*.

<Aside>
The surface covers one screen, and there is no timer, no pointer in the
picture and no scrolling capture. Window mode only sees windows on the
workspace you are on. A recording has no pause and records one take at a time.
See [limitations](/help/limitations/).
</Aside>
