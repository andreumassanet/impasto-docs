Two collections that never read each other. **A note** is a title, a body and a
colour, with no state and no date. **A task** is a line, a lane and a day it is
due. Nothing turns one into the other.

## Notes

**A note is paper**: a pastel square with the title across the top and the body
in handwriting, in a dark ink that stays dark in every palette. It looks the
same wherever it is met — in the deck, open in the island, on a square of the
wallpaper, peeking off an edge.

### The deck

<kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> opens the deck: every note as a card, the last
edited first, with **New note** in front and the keyboard's ring on it. So
<kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> then <kbd>Enter</kbd> is a blank note, ready to
write.

| Key | In the deck |
|---|---|
| Arrows | Move between cards |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> | Open the card — on New note, start one |
| <kbd>Delete</kbd> | Archive the card |
| <kbd>Escape</kbd> | Close |

Each card says how long ago it was edited, and wears a mark in its corner when
it is on the wallpaper or on an edge. **Show archived**, along the bottom,
brings the archived ones into the deck, dimmed.

### Writing one

**Opened, the island becomes the note**: it paints itself the note's colour and
drops its rim. The title comes first, and <kbd>Enter</kbd> moves on to the
body. Along the bottom:

- the back arrow;
- five colours — yellow, the one a note starts with, then the accent, green,
  blue and red — each the palette's own colour washed to a pastel, so a note
  repaints with the wallpaper;
- **Archive**, which takes the note off the wallpaper and the edges and keeps
  it — **Restore** on an archived one;
- **Delete**, which removes the note at once and asks nothing.

A line that starts with `[ ]` or `[x]` is drawn as a box, empty or ticked,
wherever the note is read, and stays those characters where it is written.
Ticking a box is editing the note, so the boxes on the wallpaper are not
clicked. Every change is kept as you type, and a note left blank and closed is
thrown away.

<kbd>Escape</kbd> goes back to where the note came from: the deck when it was
opened there, off the island when it was opened from the wallpaper, an edge or
a menu.

A note can also be started with the right button on the wallpaper → **New
note**. The handwriting is a setting: Settings → Desktop → Module settings →
**Handwriting**, off for the interface's typeface.

### On the wallpaper and the edges

A note on the desktop is read there, not written: a click opens it in the
island.

- **On a square** — the Notes widget, in any of the four shapes, shows one
  note: the newest, or the one picked on its card while arranging.
- **On an edge** — a deck of tabs along the left, right or bottom edge. The
  pointer brings them out, and the tab under it shows the whole note.

A note is in one place at a time. Putting notes on the wallpaper and the edges
is in [Widgets on the desktop](/shell/desktop/#notes-on-the-edges).

## Tasks

**A board for what has to be done.** <kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> opens three
lanes — To do, Doing and Done — with **New task** at the top of the first and
the keyboard's ring on it.

### The board

Drag a card between the lanes, or up and down inside one; a line shows where it
will land. The circle on a card finishes the task, or sends a finished one back
to To do. A day under a card turns red once it is late, and the line along the
bottom counts what is open, what is due today and what is overdue.

| Key | On the board |
|---|---|
| Arrows | Move the ring between lanes and cards |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Open the card — on New task, start one |
| <kbd>Space</kbd> | Move the card one lane along; from Done, back to To do |
| <kbd>Escape</kbd> | Close |

### A task

Opened, a task fills the panel: its line, with how long ago it was added — or
finished — beside it, and anything else about it underneath. Along the bottom:

- the back arrow;
- the day — **Pick a day** until it has one — which opens a month over the
  button: click a day, turn the month with the wheel or the arrows, or choose
  **Today**, **Tomorrow** or **No day**; <kbd>Escape</kbd> or a click elsewhere
  leaves it as it was;
- the lane, To do, Doing or Done, which is how a task is finished;
- **Delete**.

A task left without a line and closed is thrown away. <kbd>Escape</kbd> goes
back to the board, or closes the island when the task was opened from the
calendar.

### Its day under the month

**A task's day shows as a dot under the month wherever a month is drawn**: the
calendar on the wallpaper, the control centre's month, the calendar's detail in
the island. The dot is the accent while anything on that day is still to do,
and muted once all of it is done. A finished task stays on its day, struck
through.

Press a day with a dot and the calendar turns into that day's tasks. In the
island, the detail lists the day you clicked and a row opens that task on the
board; on the wallpaper, the widget comes back when the pointer leaves it, or
from the arrow.

The Tasks widget on the wallpaper, and the Tasks block in the
[control centre](/shell/control-centre/), show how many are left and what comes
first: what is late, else what is due today, else the next thing.

## Where they are kept

Notes in `notes.json` and tasks in `tasks.json`, in `~/.local/state/quickshell/`.
Neither belongs to a profile, so every [profile](/shell/settings/#profiles)
shows the same notes and the same tasks, and nothing is synced anywhere else.

There is no reminder, no repeating task and no search in the deck; the rest is
under [Limitations](/help/limitations/).
