**<kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>SHIFT</kbd> <kbd>W</kbd> opens your webcam in the
island, with effects that every call sees.** The shell feeds a virtual camera
named **impasto camera**: pick it as the camera in Discord, a browser or any
other program, and what it shows is the picture the panel shows, effects and
all.

## The panel

- **The preview**, drawn as a mirror: the picture a call gets, read back from
  the virtual camera.
- **A strip of effects** under it, moved with the wheel, a sideways swipe or a
  drag, and picked with a click.
- **Reactions**, a tile per gesture, lit in the accent when it is on.
- **The status line** along the bottom: the **Virtual camera** switch, and
  what is reading it right now. Off, no program can use it.
- **The shutter** saves the picture, mirrored as the preview shows it, into
  your camera folder (or `Camera` under your pictures), and copies it.

The webcam is on only while something reads the virtual camera, a call or the
panel itself, and the effects run only then: at rest it reads nothing and uses
no processor. While a call reads it, it takes about one core. The
[privacy mark](/shell/island/#at-rest) beside the time names the program
reading the virtual camera.

## Effects

| Effect | What it does |
|---|---|
| Natural | The webcam as it is |
| Palette | The picture tinted in the palette's accent |
| Mono | Black and white |
| Warm | A warmer picture, toward the palette's yellow |
| Blur back | Everything behind you blurred |
| Centre Stage | Closes in on your face and follows it as you move |
| Shades | Sunglasses on your face |
| Crown | A crown on your head |
| Sparkle | Sparkles circling your head, passing behind it |

Shades, the crown and the sparkles are built in 3D on the angle of your head,
so they turn with it and hide behind it.

## Reactions

**A gesture sends its own emoji up from the hand that made it.** Each one is
switched on its tile:

| Gesture | |
|---|---|
| Thumbs up 👍 | On |
| Thumbs down 👎 | On |
| Heart 🫶 | On |
| Peace ✌️ | On |
| Horns 🤘 | On |
| OK 👌 | On |
| Middle finger 🖕 | Off until you switch it on |

The effect and the reactions belong to the machine, so they stay the same
across profiles.

## Setting it up

**`./setup camera` installs the virtual camera**, and `./setup install` runs
it once on a machine with a webcam, unless `--skip-extras`. It installs
v4l2loopback with your kernel's headers, OpenCV and MediaPipe (from the AUR),
has the module load at every boot as **impasto camera**, and downloads the
face, hand and background models from Google's storage into
`~/.local/share/impasto/camera`. A machine without a webcam is skipped.

<Aside>
Until it is set up, the panel says to run `./setup camera`. When another
program already holds the webcam, the panel says it could not open it.
</Aside>

What the effects do not do is listed under
[Limitations](/help/limitations/#camera-effects).
