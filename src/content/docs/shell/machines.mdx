**<kbd>SUPER</kbd> <kbd>SHIFT</kbd> <kbd>M</kbd> opens the virtual machines in
the island**, run by quickemu. Every system quickget knows can be fetched from
the panel, with its versions and editions, and every machine kept is started,
paused, snapshotted and stopped from the same place. `qemu-desktop` and
`quickemu` are in the package list, so the install brings both.

The panel has two views behind a switch at the top: **Machines**, the ones
there are, and **New**, the catalogue. With no machine yet it opens on New.

## Machines

**One row per machine**: its system's mark, its cores, memory and disk, and
its state, with the answers that state allows.

| State | Answers |
|---|---|
| Off | Start, and a button that opens the row out |
| Running | Open its window, pause, shut down |
| Paused | Open its window, resume, shut down |
| Shutting down | A second press of the power button ends it at once |

**A machine starts without a window**, and its window opens and closes
without stopping it: close the window and the machine keeps running, open it
again from its row. The window is spicy's, with spicy's own menus.

Shutting down presses the machine's own power button, so the system inside
goes down cleanly. A system that ignores the button, such as a live ISO, is
ended by pressing it again.

**Opened out**, a machine that is off shows:

- **Cores** and **Memory**, to change before the next start;
- **Take a snapshot**, and each snapshot kept, with a cross to remove it. A
  click on a snapshot reads *Restore …?* and the second click takes the disk
  back to it. Snapshots come after the first start, since that is when the
  disk is made;
- **Delete**, which reads *Delete it and its disk* on the first press and
  removes both on the second.

## A new machine

**New lists every system quickget can fetch**, with a search field over the
list. Pick one on the left, and on the right choose its **Version**, its
**Edition** where it has several, and what the machine gets: **Cores**,
**Memory** and **Disk**, 8 to 512 GB. **Download and create** fetches it.

The download's progress takes the top of Machines until it is done, with a
cross that stops it; a download stopped half way leaves nothing behind.

Machines are kept in `~/.local/share/impasto/machines`, one quickemu
configuration and one folder each.

## On the island

**A running machine shows beside the time**: its system's mark and how long it
has run, or how many run when there are several. A click opens the panel. In
[the glance](/shell/island/#the-glance) it has its own face, with its window,
pause and shut down. It can be kept off the island under Settings → Bar &
Island → Beside the time → **Virtual machines**.

The [`impasto` command](/reference/command/) starts, pauses and stops them from
a terminal too.

<Aside>
Without quickemu installed the panel says so: `./setup packages` installs it.
</Aside>

What the panel does not do is listed under
[Limitations](/help/limitations/#virtual-machines).
